The files key.pem and cert.pem in this directory are PUBLICLY ACCESSIBLE
hard-coded credentials for demonstration purposes only.

⚠️  NEVER USE THESE IN PRODUCTION ⚠️

PROPER USAGE:

Before running the sslecho demo, generate fresh keys:

  $ ./generate_keys.sh

This will create ephemeral key.pem and cert.pem files for testing.

For production, use properly issued certificates from a trusted CA.
